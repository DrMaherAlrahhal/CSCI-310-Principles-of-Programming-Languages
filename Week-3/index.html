1 · Imperative vs Functional

Same problem, different way of expressing the solution.

Imperative: change a variable

x = 5
x = x + 3
print(x)

A sequence of state-changing steps.

Functional: transform inputs

(+ 5 3)

An expression computes a result.

4 · Local Binding with let

Local names exist inside their let expression. Nested let can shadow a name.

Two local variables

(let ((x 10) (y 20))
  (+ x y))
Change x and y.

Shadowing experiment

(let ((x 5))
  (let ((x 10))
    (+ x 2)))
Try 10, 20, or −2.
